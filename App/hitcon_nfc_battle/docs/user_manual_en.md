## 1. Quick Start

When using the App for the first time, complete the following six steps to sign in, design your card, and pair your Badge. You will then be ready to exchange cards. This section is a quick walkthrough; see the corresponding sections later in this manual for troubleshooting and full feature details.

### 1.1 Download and sign in with your personalized email

![A hacker cat uses the personalized conference email to download the App, then signs in with a Token or QR Code](images/user_manual/published/quick-start-01-download-sign-in.png)

On your phone, open the personalized link in the “活動搶先報” (“Event Preview”) email to install the App. Then return to the same email and sign in with the Token, scan the QR Code with the camera, or import the QR image. The download link only takes you to the installation page; it does not sign you in automatically.

> **Security reminder:** Your Token and sign-in QR Code are personal credentials. Do not forward them, post them publicly, or let anyone photograph them.

### 1.2 Set up your digital card

![A hacker cat sets up its digital card in the App](images/user_manual/published/quick-start-02-card-setup.png)

Set your name, image, bio, website, attributes, color, and preview in order. Only the display name is required. Check the finished card on the preview screen before continuing. You may skip Badge pairing in Step 8 for now.

> **Note:** Skipping pairing only postpones it. You must still complete Step 1.4 before other attendees can collect your card.

### 1.3 Locate the NFC area on your Badge

![A hacker cat points to the NFC area near the lower center on the back of the physical Badge](images/user_manual/published/quick-start-03-nfc-location.png)

Turn over your physical Badge and find the round `H` symbol near the lower center. This is the NFC area. Hold your phone close to this location when pairing or exchanging cards.

> **Do not scan the wrong code:** The `AGENDA` QR Code near the top of the back opens the event schedule. It is not the QR Code for pairing or collecting cards.

### 1.4 Pair your NFC Badge

![A phone's NFC area is aligned with the lower center on the back of the Badge for pairing](images/user_manual/published/quick-start-04-pair-badge.png)

In Step 8 of first-time setup, tap “PAIR TAG,” or open “My Card” and tap “Pair NTAG Badge.” Hold the phone's NFC area against the lower center on the back of the Badge. Do not move it until the App confirms that pairing is complete.

> **Note:** Pairing writes to the Tag, links it to your account, and applies protection. If the App says the Tag is locked, cannot be written, or could not be paired through the API, stop retrying and ask for help at the event team booth.

### 1.5 Tap physical Badges to exchange cards

![A black hacker cat taps a phone against a gray tabby cat's physical Badge](images/user_manual/published/quick-start-05-exchange-cards.png)

Hold your phone close to the NFC area on the back of the other attendee's Badge, then wait for the App to finish verification and synchronization. One tap gives only the scanning attendee the other person's card. To exchange cards in both directions, A must scan B, and then B must scan A.

> **Important:** You must tap the physical Badge to collect a card. The sign-in QR Code, `AGENDA` QR Code, and player website do not add cards to your collection.

### 1.6 Collect Sponsor and Community cards and redeem a prize

![The Sponsor and Community card collection reaches 10 out of 10, and the attendee visits the event team booth to redeem a prize](images/user_manual/published/quick-start-06-booth-cards.png)

After interacting with staff at Sponsor or Community booths, tap their physical Badges to collect their cards. Progress is the combined number of unique identities from both categories. Once you reach the target shown in the App, bring your paired Badge to the event team booth for verification and prize redemption.

> **Target reminder:** The App's default target is 10, but the final target and prize eligibility are determined by the current App screen, the server result, and on-site announcements.

## 2. Before You Begin

Prepare the following:

- The personalized sign-in email sent to your registration email address by the conference.
- An Android phone or iPhone with a working internet connection.
- NFC enabled on your phone.
- The NFC Badge issued for this event, containing an NTAG.
- Camera or photo-library permission, which may be needed to scan or import the sign-in QR Code and to import an image for your card.

| Feature | Internet required | NFC required |
| --- | :---: | :---: |
| Sign in and sync your profile | Yes | No |
| Set up or update your card | Yes | No |
| Pair your Badge | Yes | Yes |
| Collect other attendees' cards | Yes | Yes |
| View your cached collection | No | No |
| Submit a print request | Yes | No |
| Check prize results | Yes | No |

## 3. Download and Sign In

For a visual guide, see “[Quick Start: Download and sign in with your personalized email](#11-download-and-sign-in-with-your-personalized-email).”

### 3.1 Use your personalized sign-in email

1. On your phone, open the sign-in email sent to your registration email address by the conference.
2. Tap the personalized link in the email.
3. Follow the page instructions to install the App from Google Play or TestFlight.
4. Open the App, return to the same sign-in email, and authenticate with its Token or QR Code.

The download link takes you to the correct App installation page, but **does not transfer your sign-in identity into the App automatically**. You must still paste the Token, scan the QR Code with the camera, or import the QR image on the App's sign-in screen.

If you open the download page on a computer, it displays a QR Code. Scan it with your phone to continue.

### 3.2 Sign-in methods

- **Paste Token:** Paste the sign-in Token from the email into the “Login token” field, then tap “Sign in.”
- **Scan QR:** Tap “Scan QR,” then position the QR Code from the sign-in email inside the frame.
- **Import QR:** If the QR Code is saved as an image, import it from your photo library.
- **Open email app:** Return to your registration email account and find the conference sign-in email.

> The App screenshots below were captured with a staging test account. Your profile, card, and progress will be different.

![App sign-in screen with Token, Scan QR, and Import QR options](images/user_manual/published/screenshot-login.png)

### 3.3 Sign-in notes

- Your sign-in Token and QR Code are personal credentials. Do not forward them, post them publicly, or let anyone photograph them.
- The Token must contain a valid user identity and must not be expired.
- If the App says “This sign-in token has expired,” use the most recent sign-in email sent by the conference.
- If the QR image cannot be read, make sure the entire code is visible, sharp, and not cropped around the edges. You can also paste the Token directly.
- The App does not currently provide a sign-out button for general attendees. If you sign in with the wrong account, ask event staff for assistance.

## 4. Set Up Your Card for the First Time

For a visual guide, see “[Quick Start: Set up your digital card](#12-set-up-your-digital-card).”

> The Quick Start comic is a conceptual illustration of card setup. The actual App separates the name, image, bio, website, attributes, color, and preview into individual steps. Step `7 / 8` shows only the complete card preview; it does not show every editing field at once.

After signing in, the “PLAYER SETUP” screen opens and shows your current progress as `n / 8` at the top. Only the display name is required. You can keep the default values or leave the other fields empty and continue. Step 8 provides an explicit “SKIP” option.

| Step | Setting | Description |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | Display name | The name shown on your card and on the leaderboard; required. |
| 2 | Image | Choose a default avatar, draw your own, or import an image. |
| 3 | Bio | A short introduction shown in your card details. |
| 4 | Website | Add a personal website or social link, or leave it blank. |
| 5 | Attributes | Select up to three Emoji attributes. |
| 6 | Color | Select the main color of your card. |
| 7 | Preview | Review the complete card image, text, and colors. |
| 8 | NFC TAG | Pair your Badge, or skip this step for now. |

![Digital card preview in Step 7 of Player Setup](images/user_manual/published/screenshot-setup-preview.png)

### 4.1 Image sources and limits

- **Default avatar:** Choose a pixel-art hacker cat, dog, dragon, rabbit, seal, or another available character.
- **Draw your own:** Open the pixel-image editor directly.
- **Import image:** PNG, JPG, GIF, and WebP files under 20 MB are supported.
- The final image is converted to `48 × 48` pixels. Transparent areas are placed on a white background when exported.

### 4.2 Website rules

- Only secure, public HTTPS URLs are allowed.
- The App attempts to add `https://` automatically.
- Local addresses, IP addresses, `.local` domains, and URLs containing a username or password are not accepted.
- During first-time setup, the field accepts up to 120 characters.
- Do not link to scams, malware, infringing material, or other harmful content.

### 4.3 Finish setup

Each step attempts to synchronize with the server. After the final step is completed and saved successfully, the App opens the home screen. If the event organizers have configured a help URL, the App also asks whether you want to view the user guide.

If you switch phones or reinstall the App, the eight-step setup may appear again. Confirm that the loaded profile is correct, and do not pair a different Badge if you are unsure.

## 6. Pair Your NFC Badge

Pairing writes your personal card URL to the Badge, links the physical Tag UID to your account, and applies protection. Once pairing is complete, other attendees can scan your Badge to collect your card.

### 6.1 Locate the NFC area

For an illustration, see “[Quick Start: Locate the NFC area on your Badge](#13-locate-the-nfc-area-on-your-badge).”

The object on the right side of the illustration is the **back of the physical Badge, not a phone screen**. The round `H` symbol near the lower center is the NFC area. The `AGENDA` QR Code near the top opens the event schedule; it is **not** used for pairing or collecting cards.

### 6.2 Pairing steps

1. Tap “PAIR TAG” in Step 8 of first-time setup, or open “My Card” → “Pair NTAG Badge.”
2. Make sure NFC is enabled and your phone has a stable internet connection.
3. Hold the phone's NFC area close to the lower center on the back of the Badge.
4. Keep the phone and Badge still until the App confirms that pairing is complete.
5. After returning to “My Card,” the Tag UID should appear below the pairing button.

> On an iPhone, the NFC area is usually near the top of the phone. Its location varies among Android phones, but it is often around the center or upper half of the back. If nothing happens, slowly move the phone around the Badge's NFC area to find the correct position.

### 6.3 If pairing fails

- Do not move the Badge away during pairing.
- Make sure you are using your own Badge and that its Tag is a supported NTAG213, NTAG215, or NTAG216.
- Close other apps that are using NFC, wait a few seconds, and try again.
- If the screen reports an API pairing failure, a locked Tag, or a write failure, stop retrying and bring the Badge to the event team booth.
- The “Unlock NTAG” function for general attendees may currently be disabled. Do not treat unlocking as part of the normal pairing procedure.

## 7. Scan Badges and Collect or Exchange Cards

For a visual guide, see “[Quick Start: Tap physical Badges to exchange cards](#15-tap-physical-badges-to-exchange-cards).”

### 7.1 Collect a card

1. Ask the other attendee to present their paired physical Badge.
2. Hold your phone's NFC area close to the lower center on the back of their Badge.
3. Wait while the App displays the following stages:
   - TAG signal detected
   - Verifying physical card
   - Synchronizing card data
   - Card locked
4. When successful, the App opens a preview of the new card and adds it to your collection.

### 7.2 Differences between Android and iPhone

| Platform | Procedure |
| --- | --- |
| Android | Normally, tap the Badge directly with your phone. The system opens the App and verifies the physical Tag. |
| iPhone | On the “Cards” page, you can tap “Scan” at the lower right and hold the top of the iPhone close to the Badge. If the App was opened from an external link, it asks you to tap the same Badge again to obtain the physical UID. |

If the second iPhone scan detects a different Badge, the App cancels the collection. Start over and scan the same Badge again.

### 7.3 A card exchange is not automatically two-way

Each scan adds the scanned person's card only to the collection of the person holding the phone. To exchange cards with each other:

1. A scans B's Badge with A's phone.
2. B scans A's Badge with B's phone.

Scanning the same user repeatedly does not add duplicate cards or progress. If you scan your own Badge, the App switches to “My Card.”

### 7.4 Do not use a QR Code or direct link to collect a card

- The sign-in QR Code is only for signing in.
- The AGENDA QR Code on the back of the Badge only opens the schedule.
- A player's card URL can open a page, but it does not contain the physical Tag UID and therefore cannot add the card to your collection.
- If the App handles the action as a followed link, it records a link event but does not add the card. Any effect on scoring depends on the current event rules.

## 8. View Your Collection and Card Details

### 8.1 Collection home

The “Cards” page first displays the local cache, then synchronizes the latest data from the server. You can:

- View thumbnails of collected cards.
- View the total number of player cards.
- View Sponsor and Community collection progress.
- Pull down, or tap the offline notice, to synchronize again.
- Use the share button to generate a square image for social media.

### 8.2 Card details

Tap a collected card to view:

- Its name, pixel avatar, Emoji attributes, and card color.
- Its bio and public HTTPS URL.
- The NTAG UID and collection time.
- The card-sharing feature.

Before opening an external URL, the App asks you to confirm that you are leaving the App. Open only websites you trust.

### 8.3 View another player's collection

Tap a player on the leaderboard to open that player's page. If you have not collected their card, collection contents may appear locked or as gray placeholders. Scan their physical Badge first, then view the page again.

## 9. Sponsor and Community Stamp Quest and Prize Redemption

For a visual guide, see “[Quick Start: Collect Sponsor and Community cards and redeem a prize](#16-collect-sponsor-and-community-cards-and-redeem-a-prize).”

### 9.1 Collection rules

- After interacting with staff at a Sponsor or Community booth, collect their card using their physical NFC Badge.
- Stamp quest progress is the **combined number of unique Sponsor and Community identities**.
- The App's default target for this event is 10 cards. The final requirement is the total shown in the App.
- Scanning the same identity repeatedly does not count more than once.

> On site, this is usually described as collecting cards from different booths, but the system actually counts unique Sponsor and Community user identities.

### 9.2 View progress

The collection home shows:

- Sponsor count
- Community count
- Required total
- Remaining
- Progress bar and “IN PROGRESS” or “COMPLETE!” status

After you reach the target, the App displays “Prize ready. Please visit the staff booth.”

### 9.3 Redeem a physical prize

1. Confirm that the App shows the quest as complete or shows that you are eligible for a prize.
2. Bring your paired physical Badge to the designated staff booth.
3. A staff member scans your Badge with the administration tool to verify eligibility and record the redemption.

The “REDEEM” button only queries the server for eligibility and settlement results. It does not redeem or dispense a prize directly on your phone. If the leaderboard has not yet been frozen, the query may temporarily return no prize result. However, if the collection home already says that you are eligible for a prize, you can still bring your Badge directly to the staff booth. Staff will verify your stamp quest eligibility according to the on-site rules.

### 11.5 Printing notes

- The barcode is your print pickup credential. Do not share it publicly.
- The App stores only the current print barcode on this device; it does not contain a complete order history.

- If the App says it cannot save the barcode on the device, take a screenshot immediately.
- The App does not generate a Word document or control the card printer directly. The workstation separately applies printer alignment corrections and handles printing.
- If on-site payment, card-purchase, or printing locations change, follow the on-site instructions.

- Score
- A “You” label on your row

Pull down to refresh the latest data. Each page shows up to 50 entries; use Previous and Next to change pages. Tap another player to view their page and collection status.

After the leaderboard is frozen, it shows a settlement snapshot. The current system marks the top 10 players as eligible for ranking prizes. The yellow, cyan, and green colors on the first three rows are only visual styling and do not by themselves prove prize eligibility. ## 13. Local Backup and Restore

Open “My Card” → “Backup / Restore.”

### 13.1 Backup methods

- Export a JSON file to the system file manager.
- Copy the JSON to the clipboard.

### 13.2 Restore methods

- Restore from a JSON file.
- Paste and restore JSON from the clipboard.

### 13.3 Backup scope and limitations

The backup contains only the current user's **local collection cache**. It does not include:

- Sign-in Token
- Personal card design
- Official data on the server
- NFC Tag pairing status
- Print barcode or print request

Restoring overwrites the local collection cache currently stored on this device. The App can restore JSON files up to 10 MB. Do not edit the JSON manually, and do not publicly share a backup containing personal collection information.

## 14. Offline Use, Privacy, and Security

### 14.1 Offline behavior

- Cached card and leaderboard data may remain viewable, but may not be up to date.
- Sign-in, synchronization, pairing, card collection, print-request submission, and prize queries require an internet connection.
- If the App says “No internet connection,” restore the connection, then tap the notice or pull down to refresh.

### 14.2 Personal information

- Your name, avatar, attributes, bio, and public URL appear on your player card.
- Do not enter your phone number, address, private account credentials, or anything else you do not want other attendees to see.
- The App allows only public HTTPS URLs. You should still verify that a source is trustworthy before opening another person's URL.

### 14.3 Credentials and barcodes

- Do not share your sign-in Token, sign-in QR Code, or complete sign-in link.
- Show the print barcode and order number only to on-site staff.
- Do not use another person's sign-in credentials, and do not pair your Badge with another person's account.

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

### I cannot sign in

1. Make sure you are using the most recent conference sign-in email.
2. Check that the internet connection works and the phone's time is correct.
3. Scan the complete QR Code again, or paste the Token directly.
4. If the Token has expired, use a new sign-in email or ask staff for assistance.

### My phone does not detect the Badge at all

1. Make sure your phone supports NFC and that NFC is enabled.
2. Remove a thick phone case or one containing metal or magnets.
3. Slowly move the phone around the lower center on the back of the Badge.
4. Use the top of an iPhone. On Android, try the center to upper half of the back.
5. Close other apps that are using NFC, then try again.

### Pairing fails partway through

- Keep the Badge close and do not move it away before completion.
- Make sure the internet connection is stable.
- If the Tag is reported as locked, cannot be written, or could not be paired through the API, stop retrying and ask the event team staff for help.

### The card was not collected after scanning

- Make sure you tapped the physical Badge instead of scanning a QR Code or following a URL.
- Make sure the other person's Badge has been paired.
- If the iPhone opened the App from an external Badge link, you must tap the same Badge again inside the App. If you started by tapping “Scan” inside the App, simply complete that Core NFC prompt once.
- Check the internet connection and scan again.
- Scanning the same person again does not add a second copy of their card.

### I collected a card, but its preview will not open

The card may have been recorded successfully while its data failed to synchronize temporarily. Return to the collection page to confirm. Once the connection is restored, pull down to refresh.

### Sponsor or Community progress did not increase

- Scanning the same identity again does not count more than once.
- Confirm that the other account's role is Sponsor or Community.
- Pull down to synchronize again. If it is still incorrect, show your App screen to a staff member.

### I cannot find my print barcode

Return to “My Card” → “Print card.” The App attempts to show the latest barcode stored on this device. If you submit another request, keep only the newest barcode.

### My theme returns to the default after restarting

This is currently expected behavior. A palette selection applies only to the current App session.

### First-time setup appears again after I switch phones or reinstall the App

The first-time setup completion state and collection cache are stored locally. After signing in again, verify your profile first. If Badge pairing or collection data looks incorrect, do not pair a new Tag without guidance; ask staff for assistance.

### Before exchanging cards

- [ ] I am signed in to my own account.

- [ ] I wait for the App to finish synchronization before moving the phone away.
- [ ] To exchange cards, both attendees scan once.
- [ ] I do not use the sign-in QR Code, AGENDA QR Code, or player URL to collect a card.

### Before printing or redeeming a prize

- [ ] I have saved the latest print barcode and order number.

- [ ] I have my paired physical Badge with me for prize redemption.
